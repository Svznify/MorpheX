         The story
        
From ReVanced to RVX to Morphe.

        
MorpheX is a community-maintained project that follows the transition from RVX (ReVanced Extended) after its discontinuation by inotia00. It now adopts Morphe as its base, continuing development through community-supported patches and tooling for YouTube and YouTube Music.

        

          Unofficial distribution. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Morphe. It exists solely to distribute pre-built APKs generated with publicly available, upstream tools.
